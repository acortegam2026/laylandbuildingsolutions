Layland Homes' newest residence is a 4,032-square-foot, single-level custom home taking shape in Rancho Soldados, in the foothills of east Tucson. Walls of glass, soaring ceilings, and an open, light-filled plan are built for both quiet desert mornings and effortless entertaining — framed on every side by mountain and valley views.

The Great Room

An open heart, full of light.

Living, kitchen, and dining flow together beneath soaring ceilings, anchored by a gas fireplace and opened by a multi-panel glass wall that disappears to the covered patio. The desert becomes part of the room.

The Chef's Kitchen

A culinary kitchen built for entertaining.

An oversized island anchors a kitchen of professional-grade stainless appliances, light oak wood cabinetry, and quartz surfaces — with a walk-in butler's pantry keeping everything close and out of sight.

The Primary Suite

A private wing, spa included.

Wake to mountain light through corner glass. The spa bath pairs a freestanding soaking tub with an oversized tiled shower and dual wood vanities in brass — opening to an expansive walk-in closet.

The Floor Plan

Four bedrooms and a study — all on a single level.

An open great room, kitchen, and dining core opens to the covered patio, with the primary suite wing to one side and three en-suite bedrooms to the other.

Life at Rancho Soldados

A gated enclave where the desert begins.

The Rincons out your window and the city just down the road. Rancho Soldados is a gated enclave in the Tanque Verde Valley of northeast Tucson, where the streets give way to open Sonoran desert. Saguaro National Park's Rincon Mountain District begins just beyond, with the shops, restaurants, and top-rated schools of Tanque Verde only minutes away.

  • Gated community with large-lot privacy
  • Bordering Saguaro National Park, Rincon Mountain District
  • Hiking & riding at Redington Pass & Tanque Verde Falls
  • Mount Lemmon & Sabino Canyon within easy reach
  • Top-rated Tanque Verde Unified School District
  • Boutique shopping & dining along Tanque Verde Road
