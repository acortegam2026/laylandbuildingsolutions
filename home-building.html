Who We Are

Everything Considered, Before You Walk In

Layland Homes is a boutique builder of luxury homes in Tucson's gated foothill communities. We acquire the land, design each home ourselves, and build it complete — chef's kitchens, walls of glass, white oak, en-suite baths throughout, and California rooms opening to the desert. There's no year-long custom process to work through. You walk in, and it's ready — held to a standard the national builders don't reach.

What Comes With a Layland Home

Every Layland home is delivered complete — the finishes, the flow, and the details already considered, so there's nothing left to figure out.

Ready to Live In

A chef's kitchen with a walk-in butler's pantry, a primary suite with an en-suite bath and large walk-in closet, en-suite baths for every bedroom, a study with glass doors, and a mudroom — all finished, nothing to select.

Built to Entertain

Soaring ceilings and open living flow through walls of glass to a covered California room — expansive space built to host and to grow into.

A Higher Standard

White oak, natural light, paver drives, and a level of finish throughout that the national and semi-custom builders don't reach — luxury, thoughtfully appointed.
