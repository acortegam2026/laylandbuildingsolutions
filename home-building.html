Acre Homesite

Layland Homes' newest residence is a 4,032-square-foot, single-level custom home taking shape on a private acre-and-a-half in Coyote Creek. Walls of glass, soaring ceilings, and an open, light-filled plan are built for both quiet desert mornings and effortless entertaining — framed on every side by mountain and valley views.

The Great Room

An open heart, full of light.

Living, kitchen, and dining flow together beneath soaring ceilings, anchored by a gas fireplace and opened by a multi-panel glass wall that disappears to the covered patio. The desert becomes part of the room.

The Chef's Kitchen

A culinary kitchen built for entertaining.

An oversized island anchors a kitchen of professional-grade stainless appliances, light oak wood cabinetry, and quartz surfaces — with a walk-in butler's pantry keeping everything close and out of sight.

The Primary Suite

A private wing, spa included.

Wake to mountain light through corner glass. The spa bath pairs a freestanding soaking tub with an oversized tiled shower and dual wood vanities in brass — opening to an expansive walk-in closet.

The Floor Plan

Four bedrooms and a study — all on a single level.

An open great room, kitchen, and dining core opens to the covered patio, with the primary suite wing to one side and three en-suite bedrooms to the other.

Life at Coyote Creek

1,000 acres of Rincon Valley, bordering Saguaro National Park.

A master-planned community of 395 acre-plus homesites with a one-story height limit and protected building envelopes — so the views and privacy of every home are preserved. Quiet, gated, and minutes from Vail and Tucson.

  • State-of-the-art equestrian center
  • Recreation center — tennis, pickleball, basketball & volleyball
  • Parks, playground & exclusive community events
  • Trails to Saguaro National Park & the Arizona Trail
  • Highly rated Vail School District
  • Gated, with large-lot privacy throughout
